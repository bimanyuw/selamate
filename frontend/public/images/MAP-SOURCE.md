# Authentication background

`auth-world-map.png` is the Wikimedia-rendered 1280px preview of **Blank map of the world.svg**, credited to Canuckguy, dedicated under CC0 1.0.

Source and license: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Blank_map_of_the_world.svg

Downloaded image: https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8b/Blank_map_of_the_world.svg/1280px-Blank_map_of_the_world.svg.png

The original cartographic image is stored locally. Neutral grayscale and opacity are applied through CSS; no AI-generated background is used on the login or registration page.
